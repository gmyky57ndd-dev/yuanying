# PM Feedback template — YYYY-MM-DD  
  
**Session**: <one-line description, e.g. "Ad-hoc chat review of X">  
**Participants**: Owner, <others if relevant>  
**Context**: <what prompted this session, 1-2 sentences>  
**Status**: Draft | Decisions frozen  
**Learning-loop role**: Capture  
  
---  
  
## 1. Session trigger  
  
What caused this conversation? One or two sentences. Be specific enough  
that six months from now, the reason is obvious.  
  
---  
  
## 2. Observations from owner  
  
For each observation:  
  
### 2.x <short label>  
  
- <The observation, as the owner said it.>  
- *Owner reasoning*: <why this matters to them>  
- *Owner decision*: <what they chose, or "no decision yet">  
  
Include verbatim quotes when the phrasing matters. Paraphrasing loses  
signal.  
  
---  
  
## 3. Decisions  
  
A table. Each row is one decision.  
  
| # | Decision | Rationale |  
|---|---|---|  
| D1 | ... | ... |  
  
Rules:  
- Every decision must be actionable or explicitly a deferral.  
- "Let's think about it" is not a decision. It goes in §4.  
- Decisions are frozen once this doc is marked "Decisions frozen."  
  
---  
  
## 4. Deferred (explicitly)  
  
Anything the owner considered and postponed. Recorded so it does not  
get re-proposed by accident.  
  
- Deferred item 1 — <reason>  
- Deferred item 2 — <reason>  
  
Deferred items stay deferred until a new PM doc reopens them.  
  
---  
  
## 5. Signals to the 元婴 (learning-loop input)  
  
This section is for the 元婴, not for dev. It captures *what this  
session reveals about the owner*, not what the product should do.  
  
Examples of signal:  
- Owner prefers shape over implementation.  
- Owner notices inconsistencies in language.  
- Owner uses screenshots as primary feedback.  
- Owner's patience for unfinished work is high when the direction is clear.  
  
Fill this in honestly. If a session reveals nothing about the owner,  
leave it empty — do not fabricate.  
  
---  
  
## 6. Produced artifacts  
  
| Artifact | Path | Purpose |  
|---|---|---|  
| ES-NNN | `docs/specs/ES-NNN-*.md` | ... |  
| Tracking entries | `TRACKING.md` | ... |  
  
If nothing was produced, write "none."  
  
---  
  
## 7. Follow-ups  
  
What happens next. Who does what. When the next PM session is expected.  
  
Keep to 3–5 bullets. If it is longer, the session needs to split.  
  
---  
  
**End of PM doc.**
